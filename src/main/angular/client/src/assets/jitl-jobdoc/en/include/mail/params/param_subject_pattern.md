## Argument: mail_subject_pattern
<article id="mail_subject_pattern">

Filters incoming mail by subject using a Regular Expression.

The filter checks if the *Subject* field of an e-mail matches a Regular Expression. Only mail matching this expression will be processed.

For filtering by a substrings instead of a Regular Expression, the *mail_subject_filter* can be used.
